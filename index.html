Break Free: 7 Signs You're Living on Someone Else's Programming

Before you begin

Most of what you believe about yourself was installed, not chosen. Your fears, your limits, the voice that says "that's not for people like me" were handed to you by family, school, religion, media and old pain, long before you could question them.

That installed layer is your programming. It isn't evil and it isn't you. It's a set of reflexes running in the background, and most people live their whole lives without noticing it's there.

This guide does two things. It shows you seven signs the program is running your life, and it gives you a simple 7-day practice to start rewriting it. Read it slowly, answer the questions honestly, and do the practice. Awareness is where breaking free starts.

The 7 signs

  1. You react before you choose.

    Someone says the wrong thing and you've snapped back before you decided to. A reflex that fires faster than thought was installed, not chosen.

    Ask yourselfWhat triggered me this week, and whose voice did my reaction sound like?

  2. You chase approval you never agreed to want.

    The degree, the title, the house, the look. Some goals are truly yours. Many were handed to you as "success" before you could question them.

    Ask yourselfIf no one would ever know, which of my goals would I still want?

  3. The same story keeps repeating.

    Different people, different places, the same outcome. When a pattern follows you everywhere, the common factor is the script you're running.

    Ask yourselfWhat situation keeps showing up in my life, and what do I always do in it?

  4. You need a side to feel safe.

    Left or right, us or them. Programming loves a team, because a person who belongs to a side stops thinking for themselves.

    Ask yourselfWhich opinions do I hold mostly because my group holds them?

  5. Your mind replays the past or rehearses the future.

    Old conversations on loop, imaginary arguments that haven't happened. The program keeps you anywhere but here, because the present moment is where you can change it.

    Ask yourselfHow much of today did I actually spend in today?

  6. You feel guilty for resting and anxious when you're still.

    If stillness feels dangerous, you were taught that your worth equals your output. You are not a machine with a quota.

    Ask yourselfWho first taught me I had to earn my right to rest?

  7. You believe the voice in your head is you.

    This is the deepest one. The commentary that judges, worries and compares is not who you are. It's the program narrating. The part of you that can notice that voice is the real you, the I AM.

    Ask yourselfRight now, who is listening to my thoughts?

The 7-day Break Free practice

One small exercise a day, about 10 minutes each. Do them in order; each one builds on the last. Tick each day as you go. Your progress is saved on this phone.
